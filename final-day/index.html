Day 1 · 2026-10-14

The upset: 9z beat Aurora 2-0.

Aurora, ranked sixth on HLTV at the time of the event week, lost its opening series to 9z and was pushed straight into an elimination match. The other three openers went to the higher-ranked side. FURIA beat BETBOOM 2-0 in the only group match that later became a final-day rematch.

Day 2 · 2026-10-15

Aurora stabilised; 9z took Falcons to three maps.

Aurora won its elimination match 2-0 against Ninjas in Pyjamas. 9z lost 1-2 to Falcons after winning map two, so the Day-1 result did not repeat and Falcons took the group's top seed. BETBOOM and FURIA both won their group-B matches 2-1.

Climbing the leaderboard

Contrarian, not stupid

Screen, not a probability change
  1. 01

    Take the favourite by default. A contrarian pick is an exception that must earn its place, never a style.

  2. 02

    Only take an underdog when an external two-sided book prices it at 30% or more. Below that the expected points cost outweighs the leaderboard gain — at the observed Pinnacle price BETBOOM sits at 29.3%, just under the screen, so the rule returns FURIA.

  3. 03

    Require a verified structural story: a recent roster change with an onboarding period already served, a demonstrated map-pool edge, or a rested side against a side that just played a long series. 'They look due' is not a story.

  4. 04

    Never convert the story into a number. Roster, rest and momentum are discussed in words; no probability on this site is adjusted by them.

  5. 05

    Do not chase an upset in a third-place or consolation match. The points at stake are smaller and both sides have just lost.

  6. 06

    If the only available line is a very wide book, treat it as unresolved. A 0.20/0.84 market is a spread, not a price.

BETBOOM

Grand final vs FURIA

Case for

Ranked #14 against #4 on HLTV's analytics at observation time; arrived at the final by beating PARIVISION, Legacy and then reverse-sweeping Falcons after dropping map one. HLTV's own semifinal report notes this is Boombl4's first grand final at an MVP-eligible event since PGL Major Antwerp 2022.

Case against

Lost the group-stage meeting 2-0 to FURIA and lost both meetings in the last 30 days. Played a three-map overtime semifinal on 17 October while FURIA's semifinal was a straight 2-0.

Verdict. The only final-day underdog with a verified structural story — and at the observed Pinnacle price it still fails the screen. Pinnacle prices BETBOOM at 29.3% against the 30% threshold in rule 2, so the screen says take FURIA. If the line drifts to 30% or better before lock the upset becomes playable; at 29.3% it is a near-miss, not a pick.
