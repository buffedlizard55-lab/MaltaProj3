Methodology

How each number on this site is produced. The build script is scripts/build_site.py; the raw inputs are in data/matches.json.

  1. Match list. Every match card captured from the Hotspawn lobby (play.hotspawn.com/lobby). Times are converted to UTC. Hotspawn's own pick percentages are not read and not used.
  2. Polymarket. Two-way prices in cents. Used only if volume is at least $50 and the two prices sum to 95–105. Normalised to 100%.
  3. Stake. Two-way decimal odds from the Stake CS2 listing (German locale). Converted to implied probability (1/odds), normalised to 100%.
  4. Oddsportal. Two-way decimal odds from the Oddsportal event page, normalised the same way. Where Oddsportal names no bookmaker, that is noted.
  5. Market consensus. Equal-weight mean of the usable market sources for a match.
  6. HLTV model. Team points share from the HLTV world ranking (Oct 12 2026), only when both teams are in the top 30: points_A / (points_A + points_B).
  7. Our line. 70% market consensus + 30% HLTV model when both teams are ranked; market consensus alone otherwise. Shown to one decimal; the two teams sum to 100.
  8. Pick. The team with the higher line. Confidence: High = at least two sources and a margin of 20+ points; Medium = at least two sources and 10–20 points, or one source and 20+ points; Low = everything else.
  9. Unresolved. A match with no usable market source. These are not guessed or filled in.
  10. Name matching. Team names are matched by name or containment (for example "Team Falcons" to "Falcons"). Acronym-only matches (WBT to WROTBERRY, EAC) are not used for picks.
  11. Verification. Each captured value has a source link on the table row. Values that could not be verified are left blank and flagged ⚑.

Known weaknesses: market prices change between fetches; a 70/30 blend is a judgment call (I-13); the HLTV points share is a simple model, not a match simulation.

Probabilities are our own lines built from outside betting markets and HLTV ranking points. Hotspawn pick percentages are not used. Not financial or betting advice. Snapshot is not auto-refreshed (see Flagged for Review, I-16).
