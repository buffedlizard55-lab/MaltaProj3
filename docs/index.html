Limitations

  • Snapshot date is 2026-10-14; the fetch tool returns no timestamp, so the lobby and prices may have moved since capture. This site is not auto-refreshed; a 4–8 hour refresh is not scheduled (I-16).
  • Polymarket prices changed between fetches for several markets (I-11). Only markets with $50+ volume and an implied total of 95–105% are used.
  • Pinnacle and Thunderpick were not usable from this environment (I-08, I-09).
  • Team names differ across sources; acronym-only matches (for example WBT for WROTBERRY) are not used for picks (I-10).
  • Lobby times are shown in UTC, converted from the ET snapshot (+4h). Cross-checked against Polymarket and Stake times (I-06).
