Contrarian, not reckless

Default: retain the higher-probability pick. With equal points for either winner, expected base points are probability × match points. Choosing a less likely winner reduces expected base points; it can be a tournament-risk choice, not a proven edge. Opponent ownership, exact streak state and leaderboard position are not collected, so no leaderboard-gain forecast or automatic contrarian override is justified.

Official scoring overview describes tier points and streak bonuses; it does not establish a crowd-fading bonus. Never use Hotspawn popularity to estimate win probability. A roster announcement alone is not evidence of the direction or size of a probability change.
