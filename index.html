Every match, even low-tier

How we cover academy & low-tier fixtures

HLTV → esportsbooks → model

Featured picks use strict exact-name joins against Polymarket and Thunderpick. Low-tier fixtures (CCT Play-In, Elisa BreakOut, DFRAG, VRS academy, Urban Riga, VOID Autumn Cup) may lack a verified market in the sources we can access. Absence of a retrieved line is not proof no market exists. For those, we research in order; community participation never enters our win estimates:

  1. HLTV identity — HLTV match directory confirms the fixture exists elsewhere (time/team). HTML saved for replay. No price is taken from HLTV listing.
  2. Esportsbook lines — first-party prices from Thunderpick (POST api/matches), Bovada coupon, Pinnacle guest board (guest.api.arcadia.pinnacle.com), Polymarket (CLOB books), Kalshi exchange (CFTC-regulated), and relayed bo3.gg bookmaker blocks. Each requires exact team names (or reviewed alias) + 60s start tolerance + prematch two-way winner. Decimal odds de-vigged: pᵢ=(1/oᵢ)/Σ(1/o).
  3. Valve official ratings — Valve regional standings (official repo, blob SHA verified, ≤35 days). Glicko expectation with fixed RD 75, including its uncertainty adjustment; see the calculation methodology. Each team must resolve to a unique complete five-player rating record.
  4. Own results models — bo3.gg results fitted to Valve anchors: form model (one side unrated) and network model (neither side rated, Bradley-Terry MAP, 120-day window, fail-closed gates).
  5. HLTV world ranking — HLTV ranking (e.g., 12 Oct 2026: Spirit 972 pts #1), ≤14 days old, same dated publication for both sides, 400-scale logistic. Unranked side gets floor 0.0 with low-confidence label. Last rung before unresolved.

If no source has the team (new roster, no Valve row, no HLTV rank, no bo3.gg results), the fixture stays in Unresolved picks & limitations with every source tried and the exact blocker. Model-only rows are badged Model or No line and listed separately under Picks without a betting line in this executive summary. No probability is invented as 50/50; every row links its evidence (Hotspawn + line provider or Valve/HLTV).

Contrarian, not reckless

Default: retain the higher-probability pick. With equal points for either winner, expected base points are probability × match points. Choosing a less likely winner reduces expected base points; it can be a tournament-risk choice, not a proven edge.
