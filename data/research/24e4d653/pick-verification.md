## Method, and what this does not prove

Each row is recomputed from the quotes and ratings the row itself publishes: order-book midpoints (normalised, with the 0.15 spread ceiling the pipeline applies), no-vig bookmaker odds, the median of independent consensus quotes, Valve's published Glicko transform at the row's own rating deviation, or the project's own results-fit deviation rule. The first-hand re-reads above then confirm the primary source still said what the row claimed.

What it does not prove: the historical quotes are replayed from the artifacts the publisher captured, not re-fetched, so this certifies arithmetic and provenance, not that a price was still live when you read the page. For three rows the source was also re-read live, and the values matched exactly.
