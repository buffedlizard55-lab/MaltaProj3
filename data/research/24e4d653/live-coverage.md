- A dated observation, not a live feed: the inventory is republished every four hours, and fixtures Hotspawn adds between two publishes are absent until the next one.
- The nine absent fixtures carry no probability here by design — this record names the gap, it does not price it. HLTV identity and esportsbook lines for them are collected by the next publish run.
- The past-start block of the Hotspawn API response (records that already started) was not re-enumerated; only the future-start window was compared, because only future fixtures can carry a prematch pick.
- Hotspawn best-of format is not published in the fixture feed; the field stays blank.
- Hotspawn match URLs for the ten carried fixtures are the published rows' own source_url values, reused verbatim; the nine absent fixtures' URLs were read directly off their Hotspawn tournament pages rather than rebuilt from a slug rule.
