- Rendered Polymarket cards were discovery evidence only; saved first-party Gamma/CLOB books and the existing spread validator are the pricing authority.
- Aggregator coefficients, AI predictions, HLTV fan votes and Hotspawn popularity were not used as independent probabilities.
- HLTV BO3 evidence was not copied into Hotspawn’s format field. Where Hotspawn has not confirmed that field, it remains blank and unverified.
