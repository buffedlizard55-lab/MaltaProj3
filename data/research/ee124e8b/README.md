## 3. Limitations and what must not be done

1. The two fixtures stay without a probability until a real two-sided
   prematch price replayable from saved evidence passes the existing gates,
   or an official ranking becomes available for the ratings model. Blank is
   correct; filling either row from a wide-book midpoint, from Hotspawn
   "% picked", from an HLTV poll, or by hand would violate policy.
2. Smarkets quotes are point-in-time and move; the JSON files record one
   observation each and are evidence, not inputs.
3. The runner cannot currently reach HLTV or, intermittently, Hotspawn; any
   new source wiring needs offline saved-evidence replay tests (the pattern
   already used by the Smarkets and reference-odds rungs).
4. The live lobby now advertises ~88 CS2 fixtures versus 19 upcoming in the
   frozen published snapshot; fixtures added after 2026-10-23T20:10Z are not
   yet reflected on the site. The rendered-lobby fallback keeps full
   inventory coverage during API refusals, and the next successful scheduled
   collection reconciles the added fixtures through the normal ladder.
