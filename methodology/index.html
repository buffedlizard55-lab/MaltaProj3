A number needs a reason

How we resolve a pick

Source facts stay separate from analysis. No Hotspawn community percentage, popularity, score or Pick’em point multiplier enters a probability calculation.

01

Verify the full match inventory

Read the public lobby, CS2 lobby and the same public fixture array used by Hotspawn’s client. Reconcile every CS2 ID. Keep old or inconsistent records in the archive rather than dropping them.

Official lobby
02

Use the primary external market

Discover CS2 games from Polymarket’s directory and paginate its official CS2-tag event API to a terminal page. A repeated page, schema change, access failure or the bounded 2,000-event limit is published as a discovery limitation. Confirm the event, exact two team names, start time within 60 seconds, unique active series moneyline, accepting-orders state, token IDs and condition ID. Read both original order books. No map or handicap markets.

Official order book documentation
03

Research only primary-method gaps

For those matches only, try HLTV’s official match directory, then original esportsbook lines. HLTV’s sibling time/team cards are replayed from saved original markup; differing provider names stay flagged, not silently aliased. Thunderpick, Bovada and Pinnacle have paired-price adapters — Pinnacle through its own public guest market board, two-way series period 0 only, with “Yes/No”, “Odd/Even” and score-grid specials rejected by participant text. GG.BET, Rivalry and Stake are public-access checks and do not fabricate prices if a validated contract is unavailable. The Smarkets exchange is read through its own public, documented trading API as an independent two-way CS2 match-winner board (session 46ade650): both team names and a start inside the same 60-second window must bind, both sides must carry a resting bid and offer, the book may be no wider than 15 cents, and anything wider than 12 cents is published as thin. HTTP 403, redirects and schema failures are visible limitations—not proof of no market.

HLTV official match directory
04

Model only supported rating inputs

If no accepted external line exists, read the latest official global Valve snapshot on or before today. Require exactly one matching team name and a complete five-player roster for each team. Reject missing teams and snapshots older than 35 days. The complete-picks fallback can disambiguate several same-name five-player rows only when a fresh, exact-fixture HLTV lineup identifies exactly one row: all five players match, or a unique four-of-five core matches with a documented one-player change and the row is labeled a roster-change identity join. Two plausible rows, a three-of-five overlap or an unbound lineup fail closed; no strongest-row guess. Otherwise publish an explicitly uncalibrated rating estimate. If any requirement fails, continue to the last-resort rungs.

Valve official standings
05

Last-resort researched rungs — or the honest blank

When the Valve roster model has no row either, three clearly labelled rungs remain before anything is published as unresolved. First, our own network line: a Bradley–Terry fit over finished bo3.gg series in a bounded 120-day window, anchored on official Valve points, that fails closed — and refuses to price — whenever its graph, anchor count, per-side sample size or uncertainty gates are not met. Second, the HLTV world-ranking research model: if at least one team appears on the same dated official HLTV weekly ranking publication (no older than 14 days), the published points go through the same 400-scale logistic transform as the Valve model — an uncalibrated research estimate, never a betting line. When exactly one side has a published row, the unranked side is given HLTV’s floor of 0.0 points and the row is labelled ‘unranked floor’ with a low-confidence limitation; when neither side has a row the fixture stays unresolved. Third, wide-book indicative guidance: when the only observed two-sided books are far wider than every spread ceiling, the narrowest such book’s midpoint is published as guidance only, unmistakably badged and never counted as a money-backed pick. If even that fails, the match is published as unresolved with its full research trail.

Our calculations, not source facts

From observations to percentages

Prediction-market estimate

For each outcome, take the best positive-size bid and ask from its own book. The midpoint is m = (best bid + best ask) / 2. Normalize the paired midpoints with P(A) = mA / (mA + mB) and P(B) = 1 − P(A). Require valid two-sided books, a spread of no more than 12 cents per outcome in the featured exact-name path, matching token/condition IDs, and source timestamps no older than eight hours. The fallback origination ladder runs only when the featured method produced no pick; it accepts a two-sided book up to 15 cents and flags anything wider than 12 cents as thin, so a marginal, two-sided book is used with an explicit limitation instead of being silently promoted or discarded. Directory percentages and Gamma’s last-price defaults are not used.

These are indicative midpoints, not guaranteed execution quotes or “true” win probabilities. Trading fees, slippage and position size are excluded. A single prediction market is not independent bookmaker consensus. Inspect the official book schema

Original bookmaker estimate

For a verified pair of decimal odds dA, dB, calculate qA = 1/dA, qB = 1/dB, then remove proportional margin with P(A) = qA / (qA + qB). Only active, two-outcome, prematch match-winner lines are accepted. Implied-probability sums outside 0.85–1.35 are rejected as a data-quality policy. Different mirrors of one book count as one source family; independent families, if present, are combined using the median normalized probability.

Valve-rating research estimate

The published Valve model uses a Glicko-style expected score. We implement g = 1 / √(1 + 3(ln(10)/400)² × RD² / π²) and P(A) = 1 / (1 + 10^(g × (RB − RA) / 400)). Points RA, RB are sourced facts; RD = 75 is a fixed assumption following Valve’s fit example, not a measured uncertainty for today’s roster.

Important model limitations

This deployment has not been backtested or calibrated. A historic name-matched roster is not proof of today’s lineup. Stand-ins, roster changes, map vetoes, team form since the snapshot and bookmaker information are not added without verification. This model does not override a valid primary market pick and is never labeled a verified betting line.

Our own network line (research fit)

Some low-tier fixtures have no money line, no aggregated reference and no complete official rating rows — but the brief still asks us to originate our own line. For those fixtures only, a maximum-a-posteriori Bradley–Terry fit over finished series published by bo3.gg (a bounded 120-day window) places both teams on the same 400-point logistic scale through anchored teams that hold official Valve points; teams without official points share a division-anchor median with a weak prior and are labelled unanchored. This rung never overrides a money line, an aggregated reference price or a complete official-row estimate, and it never publishes anything when its gates fail: fewer than 300 usable series in the graph, fewer than 10 anchored teams, fewer than 6 finished series for either side in the window, or a rating-difference one-sigma interval wider than 300 points all leave the fixture to the next rung, with the published reason. Every priced row lists its two rating inputs, sample sizes, standard error, interval and evidence hash so the arithmetic can be replayed. It is a research model, never a bookmaker price.

HLTV world-ranking research estimate

Many low-tier teams never appear in Valve’s regional standings, but they do appear in HLTV’s official weekly world ranking. For fixtures where every money source, the aggregated reference, the Valve roster model and our own network line all failed, the teams’ published HLTV points go through the same 400-scale logistic transform: P(A) = 1 / (1 + 10^((points_B − points_A) / 400)). Both teams must appear on the same dated publication, read from the saved official ranking pages, and the publication must be no older than 14 days. When exactly one side has a published row — the common low-tier case — the unranked side is assigned HLTV’s floor of 0.0 points, the estimate is labelled unranked floor and carries an explicit low-confidence limitation; a floor is an assumption, not a measured rating. If neither side has a row the estimate is refused and the fixture stays unresolved. This is the same evidence class as the Valve rung — an explicitly uncalibrated research model, not betting odds. It never overrides a money, aggregated or Valve estimate, and it does not confirm today’s lineup, stand-ins, form or veto. Every input row links its own HLTV ranking page.

Wide-book indicative guidance (last resort)

Some low-tier fixtures are listed on an exchange, but the only two-sided books are so wide (observed spreads up to 0.90) that they fail every published acceptance ceiling. The brief still asks for a researched percentage, so when — and only when — every compliant rung above failed, the midpoint of the narrowest observed too-wide book is published as guidance only. It is badged in amber everywhere it appears, never counted as a money-backed pick, never used by the leaderboard plan’s upset rules, and each row shows the exact bid/ask spread it came from. An exactly even midpoint shows “No edge · Even” instead of a fabricated side. Guidance is a last-resort observation of thin money — not a validated price.

Our fair line and the pick

The fair decimal line is 1/P; it is a calculated reference, not odds offered by any bookmaker. The suggested team has the higher unrounded probability. An exactly equal estimate has no directional pick unless the ranking provider itself publishes an order for the two teams: in that case the pick is the side the same official publication ranks higher, the row records the rule and both published positions, and neither probability changes. Without such a published order the side stays blank and the fixture is listed on Unresolved picks & limitations. Missing data is not replaced with 50/50. Displayed percentages are rounded to one decimal place; the downloadable data retains higher precision.

Operational boundaries

Freshness, identity & blank values

  • Refreshes are scheduled every four hours in UTC. GitHub Actions schedules are best-effort, not a real-time service-level guarantee.
  • The executive summary counts money-line picks and research-model picks separately, prints both teams' probability on every row, and publishes every model-only pick with the money sources that failed in Picks without a betting line. A research model is never presented as a betting line.
  • The inventory observation time, price observation time, source book timestamp and rating snapshot date remain distinct. A failed refresh never resets successful-observation timestamps.
  • After eight hours, the browser displays a stale warning and disables “current pick” language. After a fixture’s explicit lock time, it is labeled closed for picks. A past scheduled start is not treated as a verified final result.
  • Matching is Unicode/case/whitespace-equivalent exact naming plus start time within 60 seconds. Abbreviations, sponsor aliases and fuzzy matches are not guessed. Ambiguous Valve names are rejected unless the complete-picks fallback replays a fresh, exact-fixture HLTV proof matching exactly one entire five-player roster, or exactly one four-of-five core with a documented one-player change (labeled as a roster-change identity join). Two plausible rows, a three-of-five overlap or an unbound lineup still fail closed.
  • Unverified factual values remain blank and labeled “unverified.” Calculated probabilities and fair lines are visibly identified as analysis; they are not filled-in source facts.
  • Access controls, robots restrictions and challenges are not bypassed. No login, paid API key, user input or betting account is required for this read-only dashboard.
  • Every published probability is replayed from saved first-party evidence before deployment. Every match row links back to Hotspawn; pricing and model inputs carry their own original-source links.
