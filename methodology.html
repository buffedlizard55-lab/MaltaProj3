Audit trail

How the dashboard decides what it can publish.

The directory is deliberately conservative. A match can be included because Hotspawn lists it, but a probability only appears after a directly reviewable external match-winner market is matched to that exact pairing.

1

Collect the schedule

The public Hotspawn Pick’ems lobby is the coverage scope. The public Hotspawn matches schedule is used to retain explicit UTC schedule information where available.

2

Check CS2 context

HLTV match pages are used as a first context check when an exact page can be verified: event, pair, format, time, roster and available stats can be reviewed there. HLTV voting percentages are never used as a probability input.

3

Capture external prices

The first automated resolver checks the public Polymarket CS2 market feed for an exact two-team match-winner market. It then records the source URL, price timestamp and outcome names. The initial snapshot also verifies selected published Stake sportsbook pages.

4

Publish or leave blank

A market pick is the side with the higher recorded external win probability. If a resolver cannot match an exact market or source, probability cells stay blank, the pick says Unresolved, and the limitation moves to Flagged for Review.

Definitions

Verification rules

Verified line
An exact external two-way match-winner price was recorded with a link to the originating market. “Verified” means the displayed number was present on that linked source at the shown capture time; it does not validate a future outcome.
Prediction-market price
For a two-outcome Polymarket match-winner market, the published outcome price is shown as the corresponding team’s probability. The data refresh records the market’s own update time when present.
Sportsbook calculation
For a two-way decimal-odds line, each raw implied value is 1 / decimal odds. The two raw values are divided by their sum, then expressed as percentages. The source odds and the calculation label are kept on the match.
Unresolved
Hotspawn lists a real matchup but this site did not capture an exact independent match-winner line. No rating, head-to-head result, popularity percentage or estimated model output is substituted for it.
“Market pick”
The team with the higher recorded external market probability. It is a label for scanning the table—not betting advice, a certainty, or a claim of proprietary forecasting.
Refresh behavior
The GitHub Actions refresh job is scheduled every six hours and can also be run manually. It rechecks publicly exposed Hotspawn and Polymarket data, retains a source audit trail, and opens no account or paid feed. A failed refresh preserves the last published verified data instead of fabricating a replacement.
!
Known limitation: C-tier and academy fixtures frequently have no liquid or listed external match-winner market. The project’s source rule takes priority over coverage claims: unverified values remain unfilled. See the current count and individual limitations on Flagged for Review.
