How the plan picks are chosen

Every probability comes from our own external-line pipeline. This page only decides which side to submit. The rules file is data/strategy-rules.json (version 2026-10-16.3).

Classes

Lock underdog under 25%
Take the favorite. Picking the underdog here is a donation, not a contrarian play.
Lean underdog 25% to 35%
Favorite by a clear margin. Only a sourced, specific edge could justify the underdog, and none is checked automatically.
Live dog underdog 35% to 45%
The underdog has a real shot: about one chance in three or better. Contrarian candidate when the line is money-backed.
Coin flip underdog 45% and up
Either side is defensible. The default is the side the lines make slightly more likely.

When we recommend the underdog (an "upset play")

All of these must hold:

  • The line is money-backed (Polymarket, bookmaker or their median), not the Valve model.
  • The line was observed no more than 8 hours before the page was built.
  • The underdog is at least 30% to win in our own probability.
  • The match is worth at least 3 Hotspawn points (B tier or higher).
  • The crowd is not already on the underdog. If 10 or more Hotspawn pickers are on record and at least half of them picked the underdog, the play is skipped: it cannot move you past anyone.
  • The match has not locked. At most 2 plays are recommended per UTC calendar day, ranked by points x underdog probability x the share of the field you would pass.

Why this is not stupid: Hotspawn awards points for correct picks only, so the price of an upset play is the expected points you give up, 3+ points × (favorite − underdog probability), shown on every upset card. The upside is variance: a hit moves you past rivals who all took the favorite. A lock (under 25%) never qualifies, because its cost is high and its chance of paying is low.

Never used as a probability

  • Hotspawn '% picked' as a probability
  • Hotspawn popularity or votes
  • HLTV fan 'Pick a winner' vote
  • point multipliers as a signal of team strength

Hotspawn '% picked' never changes a probability, a class or a favorite. It has exactly one limited use, switched by use_field_for_leverage: an upset play is skipped when the crowd is already on the underdog (a sample of 10 or more pickers, at least half on the underdog), and the remaining plays are ranked by the share of the field they would pass (a neutral 0.5 weight when the field is unknown). Samples under 30 pickers are labelled small.

Left blank on purpose

  • Streak bonus formula: the rules page says consecutive correct picks earn bonus points but publishes no formula, so the cost of breaking a streak is not quantified.
  • Whether wrong picks are ever penalised: the rules page mentions points only for correct picks and no deduction for wrong ones.
