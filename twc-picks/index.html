Our interpretation · no numerical adjustment

Contrarian, but not reckless

9z has a concrete matchup case: the Day 1 win over Aurora and a Day 2 map against Falcons. The counterevidence is Aurora’s Day 2 sweep and the current favorite price. Do not double-count facts the market may already know.

BetBoom is not automatically a bad upset choice: they recovered against PARIVISION. Whether their current line makes them a credible alternative is displayed above, not borrowed from a fan vote or from tournament outright odds. Legacy’s map against FURIA is counterevidence.

Protecting expected points means keeping both defaults. If deliberately accepting more variance to catch up, limit this two-match card to one optional switch. Ownership, leaderboard deficit and streak state are unavailable, so no chance of climbing places is claimed.
