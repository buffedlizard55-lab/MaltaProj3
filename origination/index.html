Standing limitations for originated picks

  • Hotspawn participation percentages are never a probability input, and neither are HLTV fan votes. Only paired betting/market prices and official published ratings produce numbers.
  • Prediction-market and exchange books (Polymarket, Kalshi) pass the featured path only at a spread of 12 cents or less. The fallback ladder accepts a two-sided book up to 15 cents and marks anything above 12 cents as thin; wider books are rejected, and their observed quotes are shown on unresolved cards for guidance only.
  • The aggregated bookmaker rung is a third-party average published by EGamersWorld, not a first-party bookmaker quote. It is used only for fixtures the entire first-party ladder leaves blank, must be fresh and hash-matched to its captured sentence, and is rejected when both sides print identical prices or observations contradict each other. Those rejected captures are listed below with their reason.
  • The Valve-rating fallback model is uncalibrated: it is not observed betting odds and has not been backtested. It is used only when no money source resolves a match.
  • Provider display names are joined only through the verified alias registry below. Unregistered names stay unmatched.
  • Series format (best-of) is unverified on Hotspawn fixtures; estimates target the full-series winner where the market does, else the match winner as published.
  • Past-start fixtures are never given retrospective prematch picks; the source archive retains them without manufactured probabilities.

Model note

When a research model is used, the win probability is a 400-point logistic transform, the scale in Valve's own model/fit.js (fixed rating deviation 75). The inputs differ by rung and are named on every row: official Valve standings points; a results-based fit for one unrated side; our own Bradley–Terry network fit over bo3.gg finished series anchored on Valve points (session 541cd43a); or HLTV's published weekly world-ranking points. Each model row publishes its own inputs, sample size and uncertainty, and no model ever overrides a market or esportsbook price.

Start-time join tolerance: ±1 min; any disagreement beyond 60 seconds is published as a flag, never silently corrected. Thin-market threshold: spread > 0.12; rejection ceiling: spread > 0.15.
