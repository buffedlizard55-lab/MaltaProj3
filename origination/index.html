Standing limitations for originated picks

  • Hotspawn participation percentages are never a probability input, and neither are HLTV fan votes. Only paired betting/market prices and official published ratings produce numbers.
  • Thin prediction markets (order-book spread wider than 12 cents) are shown as indicative inputs only and are labeled on each row.
  • The Valve-rating fallback model is uncalibrated: it is not observed betting odds and has not been backtested. It is used only when no money source resolves a match.
  • Provider display names are joined only through the verified alias registry below. Unregistered names stay unmatched.
  • Series format (best-of) is unverified on Hotspawn fixtures; estimates target the full-series winner where the market does, else the match winner as published.
  • Past-start fixtures are never given retrospective prematch picks; the source archive retains them without manufactured probabilities.

How each pick is produced

The ladder below runs top to bottom. The first rung that verifies a full pair of prices decides the number; the later rungs are cross-checks or last resorts. Every rung is outside information — Hotspawn supplies the match list only.

  3. median consensus across money families
  4. Valve-rating research model fallback
  5. blank + flagged

Model note

When a research model is used, win probability comes from the published Valve standings points with the formula in Valve's own model/fit.js (fixed rating deviation 75). It is labeled on every row and never overrides market or esportsbook prices.

Start-time join tolerance: ±30 min; any disagreement beyond 60 seconds is published as a flag, never silently corrected. Thin-market threshold: spread > 0.12; rejection ceiling: spread > 0.35.
