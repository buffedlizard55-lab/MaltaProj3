Contrarian, but not stupid

The seven rules this review applies

Evidence required
R1

Rankings lag; map scores do not

A world ranking is a trailing average of results earned by whoever played them. Day one proved the point: HLTV #17 beat HLTV #6 by 2-0, and HLTV's own pre-match analytics had listed Aurora's advantages. Before accepting a rank-based price, check the map-level evidence behind it.

HLTV series reportHLTV world ranking
R2

Discount teams that just changed their leadership

The reported summer rebuild changed Aurora’s players and leadership. Treat that as a question to investigate with dated lineup and map evidence, not a proven integration failure or an automatic probability discount.

HLTV official transfer news
R3

Do not fade a structurally dominant upset

9z did not steal one close map: they won their own Inferno pick 13-11 and then allowed a single round on Nuke. When an underdog wins the opponent's map pick convincingly, the result carries information about the matchup, not only about variance.

HLTV series report
R4

Closing weakness is a caution, not an automatic flip

BETBOOM surrendered 8-4 leads on both maps against FURIA, and Legacy surrendered 8-2 and 12-5 leads against PARIVISION. Both facts are real and both teams still have a day-two series. Lead conversion is worth tracking, but a single day of it does not justify picking against a clear market favorite.

HLTV day-one report
R5

A near coin flip is not a pick you should feel good about

Where two verified prices sit within a few points of each other, the honest output is 'low confidence', not a confident contrarian story. On the day-two card, PARIVISION vs BetBoom traded at 52.5% / 47.5% — a five-point gap inside one map of variance. The automated picks page still shows the higher side; this review labels the confidence, and never manufactures a default 50/50.

Polymarket Gamma API
R6

Rest and schedule pressure are equal here — do not invent an edge

The calendar dates show when teams played, not how long they actually rested or prepared. Travel and actual rest hours are unverified; no equality or advantage is asserted and no rest adjustment is made.

HLTV day-one report
R7

Crowd-fading is out of scope by policy

This project never reads Hotspawn's '% picked', point multipliers or community votes, so it cannot and does not recommend picking against the Hotspawn crowd. Contrarian here means disagreeing with the external market or ranking consensus when official evidence supports it. Anyone wanting crowd-fade analysis should treat that as an unresolved limitation, not as a hidden feature.
